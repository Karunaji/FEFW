Cross-route class pairings Cross-route planning · no story spoilers

When versions of a unit are merged, the higher value of each stat is retained. Raise leftover lows only with classes inside that unit’s intended weapon family; Part 3 returns everyone to that line. These pairings are suggestions based on class growth modifiers, not guaranteed results or established community rankings.

GoalTrain across routesWhy it fits
Fast, sturdy physical fighterDreadnought + ShidoDreadnought favors Strength and Defense; Shido favors Speed and Dexterity.
Fast physical attackerWarrior + ShidoWarrior emphasizes Strength; Shido brings Speed and Dexterity.
Hard-hitting, accurate archerSniper + WarriorSniper favors Dexterity and Speed; Warrior adds a stronger Strength growth focus.
Durable offensive mageOvate + DreadnoughtOvate favors Magic and Resistance; Dreadnought helps build physical Defense.
Tank with better magical bulkDreadnought + BishopBishop's high Resistance growth addresses Dreadnought's weak spot. Its Magic growth can also help a healer build.
Faster mounted fighterBardinger + ShidoBardinger contributes Strength and Resistance growth; Shido emphasizes Speed.

If a unit has three routes: Dreadnought + Shido + Ovate covers Defense and Strength, Speed and Dexterity, and Magic and Resistance. Prioritize the third class only if its magic growth or learned abilities suit that unit.

Class growth rates influence level-up chances; they do not guarantee particular numbers. The final class still determines which weapons and spells the unit can use.
