Advanced classes

All 17 Advanced classes, sorted into four groups by what they grow while you stay in them: Speed/Dex, Strength/HP, Defense/Res, then Magic. Nearby rows overlap a bit. The +/− numbers are extra growth % while in that class, not stats you get instantly. Exams: Renown 8, around lv 35, Advanced License. Use the tab on the right to hide the skills column. Mastery stays after you leave the class.
