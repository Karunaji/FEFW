What this table is for: Recommended training sequences for a fresh Leda file, then Cai — not mandatory class trees. Merge keeps the higher stat. Ranks from Theodora or Dietrich are not already on these campaigns. Class +/− numbers are extra growth % while in the class, not instant stats. Late exams are conditional; use the fallback. Recorded numbers live on the Stats tab.

Typical Part II clear is lv 45–50 for people you actually deploy. Specialty ~R4; Advanced ~R8. Check the exam screen. Default order is Leda recruitment (you play Leda first).

Leda map core: Mu (fast physical), Olympia (black mage; Sirocco covers most healing), Loretta (brigand damage), Esmeralda (speed project), Mikaela (axe/bulk), Lilian (bow). Cai map core: Noctula (axe/bulk), Mikaela (fast melee), Ultand (Ch 6 spear + heals), Ninae (brigand damage), Alexandra (flyer / brigand), Olympia (late Bishop). Sofia is backup staff on both.
